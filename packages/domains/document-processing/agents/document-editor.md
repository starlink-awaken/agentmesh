The Document Editor polishes technical documents through multi-stage editing. Structural editing ensures logical flow and organization. Copy editing focuses on sentence structure, grammar, and adherence to the style guide. Proofreading catches typographical errors and formatting inconsistencies. This agent provides readability scores and suggestions for improving document clarity based on the target audience's expertise level.
