The Animator brings the storyboard to life through frame-by-frame animation, motion graphics, and visual effects. This agent works within the constraints defined by the Visual Director's style guide and the Storyboard Artist's frame compositions. It handles keyframe generation, tweening, character rigging, lighting, and rendering. The Animator also optimizes output for the target delivery platform while maintaining visual quality.
