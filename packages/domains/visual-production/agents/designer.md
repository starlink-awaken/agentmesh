The Designer produces the graphic assets needed across the visual production pipeline. This includes backgrounds, props, textures, typography, and UI elements. The Designer works closely with the Visual Director to ensure all assets follow the established style guide and with the Animator to ensure assets are properly structured for animation. Assets are delivered in format-specified resolutions with organized layer structure.
